## 1. Overview and Philosophy
Quantic Global Enterprises believes in fostering a continuous feedback culture that aligns individual objectives with enterprise strategy, rewards high contribution, and provides structured support for professional development and career advancement.

## 2. Annual Performance Review Cycle

### 2.1 Cadence and Timeline
The formal performance evaluation cycle operates on an annual basis with a mid-year checkpoint:
* **Mid-Year Checkpoint (July):** An informal 1-on-1 dialogue assessing goal progress, roadblocks, and growth opportunities. No formal rating is recorded.
* **Year-End Performance Review (November - December):** Comprehensive evaluation comprising employee self-assessment, peer feedback (360-degree input), and direct manager evaluation.
* **Calibration & Ratings Finalization (January):** Cross-department calibration committees review ratings to ensure consistency, fairness, and equity across cohorts.

### 2.2 Performance Rating Scales
Evaluations use a 5-point performance rating system:
1. **Unsatisfactory (1):** Consistently fails to meet basic role responsibilities and objectives. Immediate corrective intervention required.
2. **Needs Development (2):** Inconsistently meets expectations or requires frequent supervision on core tasks.
3. **Meets Expectations (3):** Fully proficient; reliably delivers quality outcomes aligned with expectations.
4. **Exceeds Expectations (4):** Consistently delivers beyond established goals, models leadership competencies, and demonstrates high impact.
5. **Outstanding (5):** Exceptional strategic contribution, pioneering innovation, and enterprise-wide influence.

## 3. Promotion Eligibility and Criteria

### 3.1 Eligibility Pre-requisites
To be considered for promotion to the next career band, an employee must satisfy all of the following requirements:
* Minimum of twelve (12) consecutive months in their current role and salary level.
* Sustained performance rating of at least "Exceeds Expectations" (4) or "Outstanding" (5) in the most recent annual review.
* Demonstrated competency performing responsibilities at the target job level for at least one full review cycle ("in-band readiness").
* Endorsement by both the direct department manager and functional Vice President.

### 3.2 Out-of-Cycle Promotions
Out-of-cycle promotions are rare and require written business justification by the Executive Committee member and approval by the Chief People Officer, reserved for critical retention risks or organizational restructuring.

## 4. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

### 4.1 Initiation Criteria
When an employee exhibits sustained performance deficiencies, fails to meet minimum role objectives, or receives an "Unsatisfactory" (1) rating, the manager in consultation with HR will initiate a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

### 4.2 PIP Duration and Structure
* **Duration:** A standard PIP runs for thirty (30) or sixty (60) calendar days, depending on role complexity and task cycles.
* **Plan Documentation:** The PIP document outlines:
  1. Specific performance deficits with objective examples.
  2. Concrete, measurable goals and success metrics to be achieved within the timeframe.
  3. Support, training, or resources provided by the company.
  4. Weekly scheduled check-in meetings between employee and manager.

### 4.3 Outcomes of a PIP
At the conclusion of the PIP period, one of three outcomes is determined:
1. **Successful Completion:** The employee has met all specified benchmarks and returns to good standing.
2. **Extension:** In rare instances with substantial progress, a one-time extension of up to thirty (30) days may be granted with HR approval.
3. **Unsuccessful Completion:** If the employee fails to meet the required performance benchmarks, employment is terminated.
