# Employee Code of Conduct and Professional Ethics

**Document ID:** DOC-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** June 15, 2024  
**Owner:** Legal & People Operations  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees, Officers, and Contractors  

---

## 1. Core Ethical Commitments
Quantic Global Enterprises is committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards of integrity, transparency, accountability, and mutual respect in all business dealings and workplace relationships. Every team member acts as an ambassador of the company's reputation and trust.

## 2. Workplace Respect, Diversity, and Equal Opportunity

### 2.1 Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination
The company strictly prohibits discrimination, harassment, bullying, or retaliation against any individual based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any legally protected characteristic under international, federal, state, or municipal law.

### 2.2 Prohibited Behaviors
Prohibited misconduct includes, but is not limited to:
* Unwelcome physical contact, lewd comments, offensive slurs, derogatory jokes, or display of offensive imagery.
* Hostile work environment behaviors including verbal intimidation, shaming, or persistent aggressive communication.
* Retaliation in any form against an employee who reports a concern, files a complaint, or participates in an investigation.

## 3. Conflict of Interest and Outside Engagements

### 3.1 Personal Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid situations in which their personal, familial, or financial relationships conflict—or appear to conflict—with the best interests of the company. Examples of potential conflicts include:
* Directing company vendor contracts or hiring decisions to personal friends or immediate family members without disclosure.
* Holding substantial financial investments or serving as an officer, director, or consultant for an active competitor or key vendor.

### 3.2 Outside Employment and Secondary Incomes
Full-time employees must devote their primary professional energies to their roles at the company. Secondary employment, freelancing, advisory roles, or board appointments are permissible only if:
1. The activity is performed entirely outside standard working hours.
2. The activity does not utilize company equipment, software licenses, confidential data, or intellectual property.
3. The employee submits an Outside Business Activity Disclosure form to HR and receives written approval from the Legal Department.

## 4. Gifts, Entertainment, and Anti-Bribery Compliance

### 4.1 Nominal Gift Acceptance Threshold
Employees may not solicit or accept personal gifts, promotional merchandise, entertainment tickets, or meals from existing or prospective clients, vendors, or suppliers, except when:
* The gift is promotional in nature (e.g., branded pens, mugs) and of nominal value (under $50.00 USD).
* Business meals or entertainment are customary, infrequent, and have a clear business agenda.
* Gifts exceeding $50.00 USD must be disclosed immediately to the Legal & Compliance Officer and either declined or surrendered for team-wide sharing or charitable raffle.

### 4.2 Strict Prohibition on Bribery and Corrupt Practices
Under no circumstances may any employee or representative offer, give, solicit, or accept any bribe, kickback, improper payment, or illicit favor to influence a commercial transaction or governmental action, in accordance with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act.

## 5. Reporting Channels and Whistleblower Protection

### 5.1 Reporting Misconduct
Employees who observe or suspect violations of this Code of Conduct, financial misconduct, safety hazards, or regulatory infractions are expected and empowered to report the matter promptly through any of the following channels:
1. Directly notifying their immediate supervisor or department manager.
2. Contacting the HR People Partner or the Chief Compliance Officer.
3. Submitting an anonymous report via the 24/7 EthicsPoint Whistleblower Hotline or dedicated portal at ethics.quantic-global.internal.

### 5.2 Strict Non-Retaliation Policy
The company strictly prohibits any form of retaliation, adverse employment action, demotion, or harassment against any employee who in good faith reports a suspected violation or cooperates in an official investigation. Reports of retaliation will be investigated independently and will result in disciplinary action up to immediate termination of the offending party.
