# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 15, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources Operations  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees  

---

## 1. Overview and Philosophy
At Quantic Global Enterprises, we recognize that personal time away from work is essential for employee health, well-being, sustained productivity, and work-life balance. This Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy consolidates vacation, personal days, and incidental illness time into a single flexible allotment of paid leave. Employees are strongly encouraged to utilize their accrued PTO throughout the calendar year.

## 2. Eligibility and Accrual Framework

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
Regular full-time employees working 40 hours per week accrue PTO on a semi-monthly basis across each payroll cycle. Accrual begins on the first day of active employment. The annual PTO accrual rate is determined based on the employee's continuous service tenure:

* **Tier 1 (Tenure 0 to 24 Months):** Employees accrue 18 days (144 hours) of PTO per calendar year, earned at a rate of 6.00 hours per semi-monthly pay period.
* **Tier 2 (Tenure 25 to 60 Months):** Employees accrue 22 days (176 hours) of PTO per calendar year, earned at a rate of 7.33 hours per semi-monthly pay period.
* **Tier 3 (Tenure 61+ Months):** Employees accrue 25 days (200 hours) of PTO per calendar year, earned at a rate of 8.33 hours per semi-monthly pay period.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees and Contractors
* **Part-Time Employees:** Regular part-time employees scheduled to work between 20 and 34 hours per week accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis proportional to their standard weekly hours relative to a 40-hour work week.
* **Contractors and Temporary Workers:** Independent contractors, agency personnel, interns, and temporary seasonal workers are not eligible for paid time off benefits. Time taken away from contract engagements must be coordinated directly with the client project manager as unpaid time.

## 3. PTO Request and Approval Procedures

### 3.1 Notice Requirements
To ensure smooth business continuity and adequate team coverage, employees must submit PTO requests through the HR Portal according to the following mandatory notice periods:

1. **Short-Duration Leave (1 to 2 consecutive business days):** Requests must be submitted at least 48 hours in advance to the direct manager.
2. **Standard Leave (3 to 5 consecutive business days):** Requests must be submitted at least two (2) weeks in advance to the direct manager.
3. **Extended Leave (6 or more consecutive business days):** Requests must be submitted at least thirty (30) calendar days in advance, requiring formal written approval from both the direct department manager and the HR Operations People Partner.

### 3.2 Blackout Dates and Operational Constraints
Certain operational units are subject to seasonal blackout periods during peak business operations. PTO requests during blackout periods will only be approved under documented extenuating personal circumstances with VP-level authorization:

* **Finance and Accounting:** The final five business days of each fiscal quarter, and December 20 through January 3 during annual financial close.
* **Engineering and Site Reliability:** Peak release windows, including Cyber Week (the Monday prior to Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday) and planned infrastructure freeze cycles.
* **Customer Support:** Open enrollment periods and company-wide product release weeks.

### 3.3 Manager Approval Thresholds
Managers hold the responsibility to approve or reject PTO requests within three (3) business days of submission. Approvals are granted based on team coverage, project deliverables, and operational requirements. If two or more team members request identical dates simultaneously, seniority of submission and historical holiday coverage will be considered.

## 4. Carryover, Maximum Accrual Caps, and Forfeiture

### 4.1 Year-End Rollover Limits
Employees may carry over a maximum of five (5) unused PTO days (40 hours) into the subsequent calendar year. Any accrued, unused PTO in excess of five days as of December 31 at 11:59 PM EST will be automatically forfeited without financial compensation, except where strictly prohibited by applicable state or municipal labor law (such as in California, Colorado, or Montana).

### 4.2 Maximum Accrual Cap
Employees may not accrue total PTO balances exceeding 1.5 times their annual accrual entitlement (the "Accrual Ceiling"). Once an employee's balance reaches this ceiling, further accruals will cease until the balance falls below the cap. Accruals will not be restored retroactively for periods during which the cap was maintained.

## 5. Insufficient Balances and Negative PTO Restrictions

### 5.1 Prohibition of Negative Balances
Employees may not borrow against future PTO accruals. Negative PTO balances are strictly prohibited under standard operating procedures. An employee attempting to book PTO exceeding their current available balance will have their request flagged as insufficient.

### 5.2 Handling Shortfalls and Unpaid Leave Options
If an employee experiences an urgent need for time off and possesses insufficient accrued PTO:
1. **Unpaid Personal Leave:** The employee may submit a formal request for Unpaid Personal Leave (maximum 10 business days per rolling 12 months) subject to written approval from their department Director and HR.
2. **Future Accrual Timeline:** The employee may reschedule the requested dates to a future pay period when sufficient accrual will have accumulated.
3. **Emergency Discretionary Advance:** Under exceptional emergency situations (e.g., natural disaster or acute personal emergency), the Chief People Officer may grant a one-time discretionary PTO advance of up to three (3) days, which will be offset against subsequent pay period accruals.

## 6. Separation of Employment
Upon voluntary or involuntary termination of employment, accrued and unused PTO will be paid out on the final paycheck in accordance with state statutory mandates. Where local jurisdiction does not mandate payout, company policy provides payout of accrued balance up to a maximum ceiling of 10 days (80 hours), provided the employee gave at least two weeks of formal notice.
