# Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-003  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** July 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Corporate Finance & Accounting  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees  

---

## 1. Overview and Purpose
Quantic Global Enterprises will reimburse employees for reasonable, necessary, and authorized business expenses incurred while conducting company business. This policy establishes standard guidelines, authorization limits, documentation standards, and deadlines to ensure tax compliance, transparent auditing, and fiscal responsibility.

## 2. General Principles and Responsibilities

### 2.1 Employee Responsibilities
Employees incurring business expenses must:
* Exercise good business judgment and prudent spending.
* Obtain advance authorization from department managers prior to incurring significant travel or lodging expenses.
* Preserve original, itemized receipts for all transactions exceeding $25.00 USD.
* Submit expense reports within thirty (30) calendar days of transaction completion.

### 2.2 Manager Approval Responsibilities
Managers reviewing expense reports are responsible for validating that expenses are legitimate business necessities, within budget allocations, properly documented, and compliant with this policy. Managers must approve or return expense submissions within five (5) business days.

## 3. Travel Expenses and Accommodations

### 3.1 Air Travel Guidelines
* **Economy Class Requirement:** Commercial air travel must be booked in Standard Economy/Coach class for all domestic flights and international flights under six (6) continuous flight hours.
* **Premium Economy Exception:** Premium Economy class may be booked for international continuous flight durations exceeding six (6) hours, with prior written Director approval.
* **Business/First Class:** Business Class travel is strictly limited to flights exceeding nine (9) hours continuous flight time with prior VP approval. First Class travel is never reimbursable under any circumstances.
* **Advance Booking:** Flights must be booked at least fourteen (14) days in advance of departure whenever feasible to secure cost-effective fares.

### 3.2 Lodging and Hotel Guidelines
* Employees should book standard single-occupancy rooms at moderately priced commercial hotels (e.g., Marriott Courtyard, Hilton Garden Inn, Hyatt Place).
* Nightly room rate guidelines (excluding statutory taxes):
  * Tier 1 Metropolitan Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): Maximum $300.00 USD per night.
  * Standard Cities: Maximum $200.00 USD per night.
* Incidental expenses such as in-room movies, mini-bar snacks, spa treatments, or hotel gym fees are strictly non-reimbursable personal expenditures.

### 3.3 Ground Transportation and Mileage
* Employees are encouraged to use public transit, ridesharing services (Uber/Lyft standard), or airport shuttles.
* Personal vehicle usage for official company travel will be reimbursed at the standard statutory IRS mileage rate (currently $0.67 per mile).
* Rental cars must be compact or intermediate category, reserved through preferred corporate rental agency partners.

## 4. Meals, Entertainment, and Per Diem Rules

### 4.1 Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem Cap)
While traveling on overnight business travel, employees are reimbursed for actual meals and incidental expenses up to a maximum daily allowance of $75.00 USD:
* Breakfast: up to $15.00 USD
* Lunch: up to $20.00 USD
* Dinner: up to $40.00 USD
Tips and gratuities are reimbursable up to a maximum of 20% of the total meal bill. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless incurred as part of an authorized client entertainment dinner with managerial pre-approval.

### 4.2 Client Business Meals
Business meals hosted for prospective clients or partners require detailed documentation including the business purpose, meeting agenda, and the full names and company affiliations of all attendees.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenditures
The company will not reimburse the following personal costs under any circumstances:
* Parking tickets, speeding citations, or moving violation fines.
* Personal grooming services, toiletries, or clothing purchases.
* Airline seat upgrades, flight club memberships, or TSA PreCheck/Clear fees (unless designated critical travel personnel).
* Spousal, partner, or dependent travel costs.
* Losses resulting from lost or stolen personal property.

## 6. Submission Deadlines and Audit Procedures
Expense reports must be submitted through the corporate expense software (Concur/Expensify) within thirty (30) calendar days of incurring the expense. Expenses submitted after sixty (60) days will require VP of Finance approval and may be treated as taxable income or denied.
