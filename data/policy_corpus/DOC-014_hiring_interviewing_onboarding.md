# Talent Acquisition, Structured Interviewing, and New Hire Onboarding Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-014  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 15, 2024  
**Owner:** Global Talent Acquisition & People Operations  
**Applicability:** All Hiring Managers, Interviewers, and People Partners  

---

## 1. Principles of Equitable Talent Acquisition
Quantic Global Enterprises employs structured, competence-based recruitment processes designed to identify world-class talent while eliminating unconscious bias and promoting equal employment opportunity across all hiring funnels.

## 2. Structured Interviewing Methodology

### 2.1 Standardized Evaluation Rubrics
All candidate interviews must utilize standardized evaluation scorecards anchored in predefined job competencies and behavioral benchmarks:
* Objective grading scales (1 to 4: Strong No, Weak No, Yes, Strong Yes) with written evidence citations.
* Prohibition against informal "culture fit" screening devoid of behavioral rubric criteria.
* Cross-functional interview panels including at least one designated Bar Raiser from an outside department.

### 2.2 Mandatory Interviewer Certification
Employees may not conduct candidate interviews until completing the two-hour "Fair and Structured Interviewing" certification course covering legal interviewing boundaries, behavioral probing techniques, and cognitive bias mitigation.

## 3. Background Verification and Pre-Employment Screening
All offers of employment are contingent upon successful completion of background checks conducted through accredited third-party verification partners, comprising:
* Seven-year criminal history record check in accordance with federal and municipal Fair Chance hiring ordinances.
* Verification of highest degree completed and professional credential accreditation.
* Confirmation of prior employment dates and job titles for the preceding five (5) years.
* Pre-employment screening is administered strictly post-conditional offer in jurisdictions with "Ban the Box" statutes.

## 4. Comprehensive Onboarding Program (The 30-60-90 Framework)
New hires participate in a structured 90-day onboarding program orchestrated through the HR Portal:
* **Day 1 to 14 (Orientation & Foundation):** Device provisioning, compliance certifications, benefits elections, and pairing with an Onboarding Peer Buddy.
* **Day 15 to 30 (Domain Integration):** Architecture immersion, initial codebase contributions or team project deliverables, first formal manager 1-on-1 check-in.
* **Day 31 to 60 (Autonomous Contribution):** Independent project ownership, stakeholder introductions.
* **Day 61 to 90 (First Milestone Review):** Formal 90-day onboarding review, alignment with annual OKRs, and confirmation of introductory completion.
