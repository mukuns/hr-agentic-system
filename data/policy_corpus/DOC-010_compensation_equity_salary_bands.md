## 1. Compensation Philosophy and Pay Equity
Quantic Global Enterprises is committed to compensating employees fairly, equitably, and competitively. Our total rewards philosophy benchmarks base salary against median market rates (50th to 75th percentile) for comparable roles within the relevant technology and business talent sectors, adjusted for geographic cost of labor.

## 2. Salary Band Structure and Geographic Tiers

### 2.1 Career Job Architecture and Levels
Every role within the organization maps to a standardized Job Architecture consisting of five professional contributor levels (IC1 to IC5) and four management levels (M1 to M4):
* **IC1 (Associate / Entry):** Developing baseline professional competencies.
* **IC2 (Intermediate):** Autonomous execution of standard domain projects.
* **IC3 (Senior):** High autonomy, complex problem solving, domain leadership.
* **IC4 (Staff / Principal):** Enterprise-wide architecture, cross-team impact.
* **IC5 (Director / Fellow):** Industry-recognized technical direction.

### 2.2 Geographic Tier Adjustments
Base salary ranges are indexed across three geographic cost-of-labor tiers:
* **Tier 1 (High Cost Hubs):** New York City Metropolitan Area, San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle, London. (100% of market benchmark).
* **Tier 2 (Mid Cost Hubs):** Austin, Chicago, Denver, Boston, Atlanta, Los Angeles. (90% of Tier 1 benchmark).
* **Tier 3 (Standard Cost Hubs):** All other approved domestic and international regional remote locations. (80% of Tier 1 benchmark).

## 3. Annual Merit Increase and Performance Adjustments

### 3.1 Merit Increase Timing and Eligibility
Annual merit increase cycles occur in March of each calendar year, effective on the April 1 payroll. To be eligible for an annual merit increase:
* The employee must have been hired on or before September 30 of the preceding calendar year.
* The employee must have achieved a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" (3) or higher in the most recent annual review cycle.
* Employees hired after September 30 will be evaluated for merit adjustments in the subsequent annual cycle.

### 3.2 Market and Equity Adjustments
The Compensation Committee performs bi-annual statistical pay equity audits to identify and rectify any unexplained salary differentials across gender, ethnicity, or demographic groups. Out-of-cycle market equity corrections may be approved to preserve retention for critical strategic talent.

## 4. Incentive Compensation and Performance Bonuses

### 4.1 Corporate Bonus Plan
Full-time employees in eligible non-commissioned roles participate in the Corporate Annual Incentive Plan (AIP). Bonus payouts are calculated based on a weighted formula balancing enterprise financial performance (60% weighting) and individual achievement (40% weighting).

### 4.2 Sales Incentive and Commission Plans
Sales personnel participate in variable commission structures defined in individual Sales Compensation Plans. Commissions are earned upon customer invoice payment and reconciled monthly.
