## 1. Safety Commitment and Environmental Responsibility
Quantic Global Enterprises is committed to providing a safe, healthful, and environmentally responsible workplace for all employees, visitors, contractors, and partners. Compliance with local occupational safety regulations (including federal OSHA guidelines in the United States and international equivalents) is a baseline requirement, not an aspiration.

## 2. Workplace Ergonomics Program

### 2.1 Ergonomic Assessment Eligibility
All regular full-time and part-time employees—whether working primarily in an office setting or working remotely under an authorized remote work agreement—are entitled to request a formal ergonomic assessment of their workstation. Ergonomic evaluations assess:
* Desk and working surface height relative to neutral posture.
* Monitor distance, tilt, and optical alignment to prevent eye strain and cervical neck fatigue.
* Chair lumbar support, armrest configuration, and pneumatic height adjustment.
* Input peripherals (ergonomic vertical mice, split mechanical keyboards, wrist rests).

### 2.2 Remote Ergonomic Stipend and Equipment Allowance
Full-time employees approved for permanent remote work are eligible for a one-time remote home office ergonomics stipend of up to $500.00 USD. This stipend is reimbursable through the standard expense process upon submission of receipts within ninety (90) calendar days of remote agreement execution. Qualified purchases include:
* Ergonomic chairs meeting ANSI/BIFMA standards.
* Electric adjustable height sit-stand motorized desks.
* Articulating dual monitor mounting arms and anti-fatigue standing desk mats.

## 3. Hazard Identification and Incident Reporting

### 3.1 Immediate Notification Mandate
Employees must report any workplace incident, injury, near-miss event, or hazardous physical condition immediately to their direct manager and the Workplace Safety Officer via safety@quantic-global.internal within twenty-four (24) hours of occurrence.

### 3.2 Investigation and Root-Cause Remediation
Workplace Operations will initiate a formal hazard investigation within forty-eight (48) hours of any reported occupational injury. Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) will be documented, reviewed with the Joint Health and Safety Committee, and tracked to verification.

## 4. Emergency Preparedness and Evacuation Protocols

### 4.1 Evacuation Drills and Building Floor Wardens
Each corporate facility maintains a designated Emergency Action Plan (EAP). Floor wardens are assigned to guide personnel during emergency alarms. Mandatory evacuation and fire safety drills are conducted semi-annually. All employees must participate in scheduled drills without exception.

### 4.2 First Aid, CPR, and Automated External Defibrillators (AED)
Corporate facilities maintain certified First Aid stations and Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) on every floor. Certified volunteer first-responder employees receive regular recertification training sponsored by the company.

## 5. Workers' Compensation and Return-to-Work Program
Employees who sustain an occupational illness or injury arising out of and in the course of company employment are covered under statutory Workers' Compensation insurance. The company operates a proactive Transitional Return-to-Work program providing modified, light-duty assignments when medical authorization permits.
