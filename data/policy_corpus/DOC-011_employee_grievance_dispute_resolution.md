# Employee Grievance, Dispute Resolution, and Workplace Harmony Policy

## 1. Objective and Dispute Resolution Philosophy
Quantic Global Enterprises is committed to maintaining a collaborative, transparent, and respectful working environment where employee concerns, interpersonal friction, and operational disputes are resolved quickly, equitably, and objectively.

## 2. Informal Conflict Resolution
Employees experiencing interpersonal tension, communication barriers, or disagreements regarding work allocation are encouraged to address the matter directly through constructive informal dialogue. If direct dialogue is impractical or unsuccessful, employees may request informal coaching or facilitated discussion from their HR People Partner or department manager.

## 3. Formal Multi-Stage Grievance Procedure
When informal efforts do not resolve a dispute, or when an issue involves allegations of unfair treatment, policy misapplication, or retaliation, an employee may initiate the formal three-stage grievance procedure:

### 3.1 Stage 1: Written Grievance Submission
* The employee submits a formal Grievance Notification in writing to their HR People Partner within twenty-one (21) calendar days of the disputed occurrence.
* The notification must detail the factual background, the specific policy or practice at issue, affected parties, and the requested remedy.
* The HR People Partner will conduct preliminary fact-finding interviews and provide a written Stage 1 determination within ten (10) business days.

### 3.2 Stage 2: Management Review and Mediation
* If the employee is dissatisfied with the Stage 1 outcome, they may escalate the grievance within seven (7) business days to the Department Vice President and the Director of Employee Relations.
* A formal Stage 2 review panel will convene with the employee to examine evidence and consider alternative resolutions.
* The Stage 2 written decision will be issued within fifteen (15) business days.

### 3.3 Stage 3: Independent Executive Review
* If the dispute remains unresolved following Stage 2, the employee may petition for final review by the Chief People Officer and an impartial Executive Committee representative.
* The Stage 3 review constitutes the enterprise's final and binding administrative determination.

## 4. Confidentiality and Whistleblower Safeguards
All grievance filings, testimony, documentation, and determinations are treated with the strictest confidentiality. Information will be shared strictly on a need-to-know basis. Retaliation of any kind against an employee who files a grievance or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary termination.
