# Whistleblower Protection, Internal Investigations, and Retaliation Prevention Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-020  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 20, 2024  
**Owner:** Audit Committee & Office of the General Counsel  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees, Officers, Contractors, and Third-Party Agents  

---

## 1. Statement of Principle and Legal Foundation
Quantic Global Enterprises enforces zero-tolerance for retaliation against any individual who raises concerns regarding unlawful conduct, accounting fraud, corporate financial misstatements, environmental safety hazards, or violations of enterprise policy. This policy satisfies reporting standards under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX), Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform Act, and European Union Whistleblowing Directive.

## 2. Protected Disclosures and Scope of Reporting

### 2.1 Definition of Protected Disclosure
A protected whistleblower report involves any good-faith disclosure regarding:
* Suspected bribery, embezzlement, money laundering, or illegal kickbacks.
* Fraudulent accounting practices, internal control circumvention, or auditing manipulation.
* Substantial health and safety perils posing danger to workforce or public welfare.
* Systemic discrimination, sexual harassment, or human rights violations.

### 2.2 Independent Reporting Channels
Disclosures may be submitted 24 hours a day, 365 days a year via:
* **The Global Ethics Hotline:** Multilingual telephone support at +1-800-555-ETHIC.
* **The Secure Web Intake Portal:** Anonymous intake hosted externally at ethics.quantic-global.internal.
* Direct in-person notification to the Audit Committee Chairman or General Counsel.

## 3. Investigation Protocol and Due Process

### 3.1 Triage and Preliminary Assessment
Within seventy-two (72) hours of submission, the Chief Compliance Officer and Audit Committee will conduct an initial risk assessment to determine jurisdiction and appoint an independent investigator.

### 3.2 Formal Investigative Procedures
* Investigators will interview witnesses, inspect computer forensics, review financial logs, and preserve physical evidence under formal evidentiary chain-of-custody standards.
* The subject of the investigation will be provided written notification of the allegations and an objective opportunity to respond prior to final determination.
* Written findings and recommendations will be submitted to the Board of Directors Audit Committee within sixty (60) calendar days.

## 4. Absolute Retaliation Prohibition and Sanctions

### 4.1 Retaliation Protection Safeguards
No employee making a protected disclosure in good faith shall be discharged, demoted, suspended, threatened, harassed, or in any manner discriminated against in the terms and conditions of employment. Any supervisor or executive found to have engaged in retaliatory conduct will face immediate termination of employment and personal civil liability where applicable by statute.

### 4.2 Confidentiality and Anonymity Guarantees
The identity of a reporting whistleblower will be kept strictly confidential to the fullest extent possible under applicable law, disclosed only on a need-to-know basis to investigators or under court subpoena.
