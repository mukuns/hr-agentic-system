# International Business Travel, Immigration, and Global Mobility Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-017  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Global Mobility & Immigration Services  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees Undertaking Cross-Border Travel  

---

## 1. Scope and Global Mobility Strategy
Quantic Global Enterprises operates across multiple global markets. This policy defines the legal guidelines, taxation rules, visa procedures, and travel emergency safeguards applicable to all employees traveling across international borders for company business or undertaking cross-border rotational assignments.

## 2. Business Visas and Work Authorizations

### 2.1 Distinction Between Business Meetings and Work Authorization
Employees traveling internationally for commercial conferences, high-level client presentations, or internal corporate summits typically enter under standard Short-Term Business Visitor Visas (or visa waiver frameworks such as ESTA or Schengen 90-day rules). 
* Performing hands-on productive technical work, local software engineering, or site installation while traveling on a tourist or visitor visa is strictly prohibited by international immigration laws.
* If productive labor or extended residency exceeding thirty (30) days is required, the employee must contact Global Mobility at least sixty (60) days in advance to secure formal Temporary Work Authorization.

### 2.2 Corporate Visa Sponsorship Protocol
The company provides corporate legal sponsorship for approved specialty occupation visas (including H-1B, L-1, O-1, and permanent residency green card filings in the United States, and Global Talent / Skilled Worker visas in the UK and EU). Sponsorship decisions are made on objective business criteria, role scarcity, and sustained top-tier performance ratings.

## 3. Permanent Establishment and International Tax Traps

### 3.1 183-Day Statutory Rule and Tax Residency
Employees must not work from a foreign country for more than thirty (30) cumulative business days in any rolling twelve-month period without prior written approval from the Chief Financial Officer and Tax Director. Exceeding thirty days risks creating a statutory "Permanent Establishment" (corporate tax nexus) for the enterprise or triggering personal local income tax withholding liabilities for the employee.

### 3.2 Shadow Payroll and Equalization
For authorized long-term international assignments, the company provides Tax Equalization Services through designated global accounting partners, ensuring that the assignee incurs no greater or lesser income tax burden than had they remained in their home country.

## 4. International Travel Medical Emergencies and SOS Protection

### 4.1 Global SOS Medical and Security Assistance
All international travelers are automatically enrolled in International SOS emergency travel protection. This benefit covers 24/7 multilingual medical consultations, emergency prescription coordination, political unrest evacuation, and global medical repatriation up to $1,000,000 USD per incident.

### 4.2 Travel Health Advisories and Immunizations
Employees traveling to destinations designated with Level 3 or Level 4 travel advisories by the World Health Organization (WHO) or U.S. State Department must obtain medical clearance and mandatory prophylactic immunizations at least twenty-one (21) days prior to departure, with all medical costs reimbursable by the company.
