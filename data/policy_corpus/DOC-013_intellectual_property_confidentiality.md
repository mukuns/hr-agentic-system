# Proprietary Information, Inventions Agreement, and Intellectual Property Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-013  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** July 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Legal Department & Intellectual Property Counsel  
**Applicability:** All Global Personnel, Contractors, and Advisors  

---

## 1. Overview and Enterprise IP Commitment
Innovation, software craftsmanship, algorithmic discoveries, and domain methodologies form the foundational assets of Quantic Global Enterprises. This policy defines the rights, duties, and restrictions safeguarding company intellectual property (IP), proprietary codebases, trade secrets, and patentable inventions.

## 2. Proprietary Information and Trade Secrets

### 2.1 Definition of Trade Secrets and Confidential Assets
Confidential Information encompasses all non-public technical, operational, financial, and strategic information disclosed to or developed by employees during their employment tenure. This explicitly includes:
* Source code repositories, architectural designs, proprietary neural network model weights, hyperparameters, and embedding pipelines.
* Customer rosters, prospective pipeline deal values, negotiated vendor agreements, and financial ledger data.
* Internal business plans, executive meeting records, and compensation calibration rubrics.

### 2.2 Non-Disclosure Covenants and Duration of Obligation
Employees must hold all Proprietary Information in strict confidence both during active employment and following separation from the company. The obligation to protect trade secrets endures indefinitely until such information lawfully enters the public domain without violation of any confidentiality duty.

## 3. Assignment of Inventions and Works for Hire

### 3.1 Comprehensive Invention Assignment
Every employee agrees that all inventions, discoveries, designs, algorithms, patentable processes, and copyrightable works created, conceived, or authored by the employee—either solely or collaboratively—during the period of employment belong exclusively to Quantic Global Enterprises if:
1. The invention was developed using company hardware, software licenses, computational resources, or facility space.
2. The invention relates directly to the current or demonstrably anticipated business, products, research, or development of the company.
3. The invention results from tasks, projects, or research assigned to the employee by the company.

### 3.2 Prior Inventions Carve-Out
Inventions conceived and reduced to practice prior to commencement of employment are excluded from company ownership, provided they are formally itemized on Exhibit A of the employee's signed Employee Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (EPIIA) at the time of hire.

## 4. Open-Source Software (OSS) Contribution Governance

### 4.1 Internal OSS Review Board (OSSRB) Approval
Employees contributing code to external public open-source software repositories on behalf of the company or utilizing company hardware must submit an Open Source Contribution Request to the OSSRB. Approval ensures that corporate trade secrets, patented techniques, and copyright notices are safeguarded.

### 4.2 Inbound Open-Source Licensing Compliance
Engineers incorporating third-party open-source libraries into enterprise commercial software products must ensure license compatibility:
* Permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-3-Clause, ISC) are pre-approved for production deployment.
* Strong copyleft licenses (GPLv2, GPLv3, AGPL) are strictly barred from incorporation into proprietary binary builds without explicit written sign-off from the General Counsel.
