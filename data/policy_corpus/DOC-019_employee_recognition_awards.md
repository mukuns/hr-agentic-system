# Employee Recognition, Peer Appreciation, and Service Milestones Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-019  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** July 10, 2024  
**Owner:** People Experience & Culture  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees  

---

## 1. Purpose of Employee Recognition
At Quantic Global Enterprises, recognizing exceptional effort, cross-functional collaboration, customer obsession, and corporate values alignment is fundamental to our inclusive culture. This policy formalizes our peer-to-peer appreciation channels, spot bonuses, and anniversary milestone rewards.

## 2. Peer-to-Peer Recognition (The Kudos Program)

### 2.1 Monthly Points Allotment
Through our integrated recognition platform (Bonusly/Kudos), every active employee receives a monthly budget of 100 recognition points (equivalent to $100.00 USD) to award to colleagues in recognition of outstanding teamwork, helpful mentorship, or exemplary execution.
* Points must be accompanied by a public recognition note citing the specific company core value demonstrated.
* Points may be redeemed for digital gift cards, charitable donations, or corporate merchandise. Unused monthly giving points expire at the end of each calendar month to encourage continuous real-time recognition.

## 3. Spot Bonuses and Enterprise Excellence Awards

### 3.1 Manager Spot Bonuses
Managers may award discretionary cash Spot Bonuses ranging from $250.00 to $1,500.00 USD to recognize extraordinary sprint performance, successful off-hours incident triage, or breakthrough client deliverables. Spot bonuses are approved by the Department Director and disbursed on the next regular payroll cycle.

### 3.2 Annual CEO Innovation Award
The highest enterprise accolade, the CEO Innovation Award, is presented annually to individual contributors or project pods whose engineering or product breakthroughs have delivered transformational enterprise value. Recipients receive a $10,000.00 USD cash honorarium and a commemorative trophy at the annual Global All-Hands Gala.

## 4. Career Service Milestone Anniversaries
The company honors loyalty and continuous tenure through Service Milestone awards commemorated at 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20 years of continuous service:
* **3 Years:** $500 milestone catalog reward selection and personalized recognition plaque.
* **5 Years:** $1,500 travel voucher and one additional week of paid sabbatical leave (40 hours) to be taken during the 5th anniversary year.
* **10 Years:** $3,000 travel experience grant and two consecutive weeks of paid sabbatical leave.
