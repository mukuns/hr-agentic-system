# Employee Separation, Offboarding, and Severance Administration Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-015  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** July 25, 2024  
**Owner:** HR People Operations & Employee Relations  
**Applicability:** All Departing Personnel  

---

## 1. Scope and Separation Classifications
This policy governs the standardized departure procedures for all employees separating from Quantic Global Enterprises. Separations are categorized into three categories:
1. **Voluntary Resignation:** Departure initiated by the employee (resignation, retirement).
2. **Involuntary Termination for Performance/Restructuring:** Departure initiated by the company (position elimination, reduction in force, performance deficit).
3. **Involuntary Termination for Cause:** Immediate separation resulting from gross misconduct, ethics violations, fraud, or intentional breach of safety/security policies.

## 2. Resignation Notice Periods and Exit Procedures

### 2.1 Standard Notice Expectations
Employees resigning in good standing are requested to provide advance written notice:
* Individual Contributors (IC1 - IC3): Minimum of two (2) weeks (ten business days) notice.
* Senior Contributors and Managers (IC4+, M1+): Minimum of three (3) weeks notice.
* Executive Leadership (M3+, Officers): Minimum of four (4) weeks notice.

### 2.2 Transition Plan and Handover Checklist
Departing employees must collaborate with their manager to author a comprehensive transition document documenting active project milestones, account credentials, ongoing vendor contacts, and stakeholder dependencies.

## 3. Severance Guidelines and Separation Packages

### 3.1 Severance Eligibility Framework
Severance pay is not a statutory entitlement but may be granted at company discretion during organizational reductions in force (RIF) or position eliminations, conditioned upon the execution of a standard General Release and Separation Agreement.

### 3.2 Standard Severance Calculation Tiers
Where severance is offered, standard schedules provide:
* **Base Severance:** Two (2) weeks of regular base salary for each completed full year of continuous service, subject to a minimum floor of four (4) weeks and a maximum ceiling of twenty-six (26) weeks.
* **COBRA Health Continuation Subsidy:** Company subsidizes 100% of the employer-equivalent share of COBRA health coverage premiums for a period equal to the severance duration.
* **Outplacement Services:** Professional career transition coaching and resume counseling for up to sixty (60) days through approved outplacement partners.

## 4. Hardware Return, Access Revocation, and Final Pay

### 4.1 Asset Surrender Protocol
All company-owned hardware (laptops, mobile devices, security dongles, building access keycards) must be surrendered to IT Logistics on or before the final active working day. For remote staff, prepaid shipping containers are provided.

### 4.2 Account Deprovisioning Timetable
All Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials, corporate email accounts, Slack access, and VPN tunnels are automatically terminated at 5:00 PM local time on the employee's final working day, or immediately upon notification for separations for cause.

### 4.3 Final Pay Distribution
Final wages—including earned regular salary, approved expense reimbursements, and accrued statutory PTO payouts—are issued on the employee's final working day in jurisdictions requiring immediate payment (e.g. California, Massachusetts) or on the next regular payroll cycle where permitted by law.
