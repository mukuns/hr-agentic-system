# Professional Development, Tuition Assistance, and Continuous Learning Policy

**Document ID:** DOC-018  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Revised:** August 15, 2024  
**Owner:** People Development & Learning Operations  
**Applicability:** All Regular Full-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Philosophy of Continuous Learning
Quantic Global Enterprises believes that continuous skill acquisition and intellectual vitality are essential to employee fulfillment and technological leadership. This policy establishes our financial support programs for technical certifications, academic degrees, conference attendance, and professional memberships.

## 2. Annual Professional Learning Stipend

### 2.1 Stipend Allocation and Eligible Expenditures
Every regular full-time employee with at least three (3) months of service receives an annual individual learning and development stipend of **$2,500.00 USD per calendar year**. Unused stipend amounts do not carry over across calendar years. Qualified expenditures include:
* Technical, leadership, or business courses and bootcamps (e.g., Coursera, O'Reilly, Pluralsight, Quantic Executive programs).
* Professional certifications and examination fees (e.g., AWS, GCP, PMP, SHRM, CISSP).
* Industry conferences, symposium registration fees, and related workshop tickets.
* Professional books, technical subscriptions, and accredited journals.

### 2.2 Reimbursement and Pre-Approval Guidelines
Expenses under $500.00 USD may be submitted directly through the expense management tool with course completion receipts. Expenditures exceeding $500.00 USD require prior manager written sign-off confirming direct relevance to the employee's career progression or team roadmap.

## 3. Academic Degree Tuition Assistance Program

### 3.1 Eligibility Pre-requisites
Employees seeking undergraduate or graduate degree credentials (such as an MBA, MS in Computer Science, or MS in Data Science) may apply for the enterprise Tuition Assistance Program after completing **twelve (12) continuous months of service** with a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or better.

### 3.2 Funding Ceiling and Grade Requirements
* The program reimburses up to **$5,250.00 USD per calendar year** (the statutory tax-free educational assistance limit under IRS Section 127).
* Courses must be offered by accredited colleges or universities.
* Grade standards: The employee must earn a grade of "B" or higher in graduate coursework, or "C" or higher in undergraduate coursework to qualify for reimbursement.

### 3.3 Post-Completion Service Retention Agreement
Employees receiving tuition assistance exceeding $2,500 in a rolling twelve-month period agree to remain employed with the company for at least twelve (12) months following course completion. If the employee voluntarily resigns prior to twelve months, a pro-rated portion of the tuition subsidy must be repaid to the company.
